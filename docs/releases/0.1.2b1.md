# Sigenergy ESS – mySigen cloud 0.1.2 Beta 1

**Before installing.** Requires Home Assistant Core 2026.11.0 or later and Sigenergy Local Modbus 2.0.3 or later with a plant set up. This is a beta: enable prereleases for this repository in the Marketplace to get it. See the [guide](https://github.com/TypQxQ/Sigenergy-Local-Modbus-mySigen/blob/v.0.1.2b1/docs/USAGE.md#-ac-charger) for the new entities.

- **AC charger.** When mySigen lists an AC charger, four sensors appear: the charging mode in use (full power, solar surplus or Sigen AI), whether battery boost is on, its cut-off level, and the energy of the current or last charging session. With **Allow battery control** on, you also get the charging mode as a dropdown, a battery boost switch and the cut-off as a number, all disabled until you enable them. Like every change, they are checked first, sent once and only count when mySigen shows them. They also work while Remote EMS is on, as the DC charger settings do. Start, stop, power limits and the charger's own readings stay in Sigenergy ESS.

Built from the readings pg1261 and cjungde shared in [discussion 38](https://github.com/TypQxQ/Sigenergy-Local-Modbus-mySigen/discussions/38): both plants gave the same mode codes and battery boost values, and showed that mySigen's "single charging power" is really the session's energy in kWh. Thank you! A change is sent the way the mySigen app sends it, but **no change from Home Assistant has been tried on a real AC charger yet**. Have one? Change one thing at a time, check the app, and tell us how it went.

Existing connections keep their settings and entity IDs. Everything from 0.1.1 is included.
